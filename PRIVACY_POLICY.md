# Privacy Policy

The full, always up to date privacy policy of the website is published at
**https://zenkord.kdns.fr/confidentialite.html**.

## Zenkord (the software)

Zenkord is a client modification for Discord. Plugins, themes, settings and keybinds run
entirely on your device and are never sent to us.

- **Updates**: Zenkord checks GitHub for new releases and verifies every update with SHA-256
  before installing it.
- **Anonymous user counter**: while Zenkord is open, it sends a random identifier, regenerated
  at every launch, every 5 minutes to our counter hosted on Cloudflare. No Discord account,
  username, message or device data is sent. The counter only keeps a salted, daily-rotated
  hash of your IP address for 6 minutes to prevent abuse. You can turn it off in the Zenkord
  settings ("Count me anonymously in the Zenkord user counter").
- **Anonymous install counter**: the first time Zenkord runs on a computer, it sends one single
  request with no identifier at all, so the website can show how many installations exist. A
  local file remembers it was sent so the same computer is never counted twice. The counter only
  keeps a salted, daily-rotated hash of your IP address for one day to limit abuse. The same
  setting turns it off.
- **Optional plugins** (AI, translation, etc.) contact the third-party services you choose to
  enable in their settings.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, do not read or store your messages, DMs or
calls, and do not sell or share data.

Since Zenkord modifies the official Discord client, all messaging, calls and server activity
still happen through Discord's own infrastructure and are governed by
[Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy/).
